## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution

FarmDirect is an AI-powered agricultural marketplace that eliminates unnecessary intermediaries by connecting farmers directly with consumers, retailers, and bulk buyers.

The platform provides:
1) Direct buying and selling of agricultural products.
2) Farmer and buyer registration with secure login.
3) Real-time product listings and price updates.
4) Online ordering and order management.
5) Logistics and transportation support.
6) AI-based demand forecasting.
7) Route optimization for efficient delivery.
8)  Admin dashboard for monitoring marketplace activities.
   
Innovation: FarmDirect combines digital marketplace technology and AI to create a transparent, efficient, and farmer-friendly agricultural ecosystem.

## Feasibility and Viability
1)Technical Feasibility: Uses affordable and widely available technologies such as Python and Django.
2)Economic Feasibility: Reduces unnecessary intermediary costs and supports affordable transactions.
